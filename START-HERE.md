# Start here

Job Buddy finds jobs that suit you, tells you how good a match each one is and why, helps you
write your CV and cover letter honestly, and keeps track of every application. This page gets
you from nothing to your first matches in about 15 minutes. No technical knowledge needed.

## What you need

- The **Claude desktop app** (Windows or Mac), signed in. Plugins need a paid Claude plan.
- Your **CV** as a Word or PDF file.

## 1. Add Job Buddy to Claude (about 2 minutes)

1. Open the Claude app and go to **Cowork**.
2. Open **Customize**, then **Plugins**, then **Add marketplace**.
3. Paste this and confirm: `adityab94/job-buddy`
4. Find **job-buddy** in the list and click **Install**.

## 2. Make a folder for your job search (1 minute)

1. On your computer, make a new empty folder, for example **Job search** in Documents.
2. Put your CV in it.
3. In Cowork, start a new task and choose that folder when it asks which folder to work in.

<!-- Screenshot to add: choosing the folder in Cowork -->

## 3. Say "set me up" (about 10 minutes)

Type **set me up** and press Enter. Job Buddy takes it from there in three steps:

1. **You.** It reads your CV and asks three things: what kind of job you want next, where,
   and anything that's a definite no.
2. **Your first matches.** It looks at real job ads for your kind of role, works out what a
   great match means for you, and shows you your first three matches. Your browser may ask
   you to **Allow** a few job sites: that's Job Buddy reading public job listings. It never
   logs in anywhere or applies for you.
3. **Tune it.** It shows you eight jobs and you say **great**, **okay** or **no** for each.
   Job Buddy learns from your answers until it agrees with you at least 7 times out of 8.

## 4. Your dashboard

Everything appears on your dashboard: what to do next, where each application stands, and
what to learn to land better roles. Job Buddy can keep it in your Claude sidebar (private to
you, and it works on your phone too), or you can double-click `dashboard.html` in your folder.

<!-- Screenshot to add: the dashboard -->

## Things to say any time

- **find me roles**: look for new jobs
- **is this a good fit?** and paste a job link
- **tailor my CV for** a company: an honest, tailored CV and cover letter
- **I heard back from** a company: update your tracker
- **what should I learn?**: the skill that would open up the most good jobs
- **how's my search going?**
- **show my dashboard**

## Your privacy

Your CV, profile and tracker stay in your folder. If you choose the dashboard in your Claude
sidebar, it's private to your Claude account and holds your job list, never your CV or notes.
Nothing is ever sent to the people who made Job Buddy.

## Something not working?

See [Troubleshooting](plugins/job-buddy/TROUBLESHOOTING.md), or ask Claude to "report a
problem with Job Buddy".

Prefer the terminal? Job Buddy also works in Claude Code:
`/plugin marketplace add adityab94/job-buddy`, then
`/plugin install job-buddy@job-buddy`, though some job sites and the
sidebar dashboard need the Claude desktop app.
