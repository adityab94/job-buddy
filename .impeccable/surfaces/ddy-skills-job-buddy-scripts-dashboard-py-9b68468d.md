# Surface: Job Buddy dashboard (dashboard.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: job seekers, many non-technical, checking in a few times a week.
Task: see what needs doing next, where each application stands, whether the rubric works, what to learn.
Constraints: one offline HTML file generated by dashboard.py; no network; light default with a theme toggle.
Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm greenhouse for a job search: frosted glass panes over a soft cream-to-sage
gradient, where the one thing to do next is always the brightest pane. Refuses the grey
admin-dashboard grid of equal cards and the hero-metric strip.

OWN-WORLD: Cream (#FDF0D5) and forest green (#4A7535) from the logo, with sage and moss
mid-tones and a warm amber for "waiting" states. Translucent panes (backdrop blur, 1px light
inner edge, soft offset shadow), pill chips for tiers, rounded 22px corners, system sans. Dark:
deep forest night glass with cream ink.

STORY: The user lands, sees who they are in their search (one sentence summary), the next
actions as the lead pane, then the pipeline, then whether the rubric works and what to learn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo top-left with "Your job search" and a one-line status sentence; theme
toggle and search top-right. Below: a wide "Next up" glass pane (left, 2/3) beside a compact
stack of four plain-language counts (right, 1/3). The pipeline begins at the fold.

FORM: Brief-pinned (soft UI + liquid glass + gradient); signature interaction: the theme toggle
crossfades the gradient and glass tint; cards lift on hover with light catching the top edge.
seed: none (direction pinned by brief)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
