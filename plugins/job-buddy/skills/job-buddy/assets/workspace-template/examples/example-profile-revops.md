# Example profile: RevOps / GTM automation (Ireland)

Status: ACTIVE
This is an example to show the level of detail a good profile has.
Your own profile will look different, especially the rubric.

## What I'm looking for
- Target role titles: Revenue Operations Analyst / Manager, Sales Operations Manager,
  GTM Systems / Business Systems Analyst, GTM Automation Lead
- Seniority: Analyst to Manager (about 5 years' experience)
- Locations: Dublin, or remote within Ireland
- Work mode: hybrid or remote
- Contract type: permanent, or contracts of 12+ months
- What makes a role exciting: building automation and AI agents into go-to-market
  processes, not just maintaining reports

## Hard filters
- Must be Ireland-based or remote from Ireland
- No contracts under 12 months

## Rubric
| Dimension | Weight | 5 looks like | 3 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core RevOps match | 30 | Owns CRM, quote-to-cash, forecasting or routing end to end | RevOps is part of a broader ops/admin role | Sales ops in name only; mostly reporting or data entry |
| Automation & tooling | 25 | Salesforce plus workflow tools (Zapier, HubSpot, Marketo) central to the job | Uses the tools, someone else builds | No real systems work |
| AI / agent signal | 15 | Building or running AI agents / LLM workflows is in scope | AI mentioned as a nice-to-have | No AI element |
| Seniority fit | 20 | Analyst-to-Manager band, ~5 years expected | Slightly junior, or a stretch up | Entry level or Director+ |
| Location fit | 10 | Dublin hybrid or fully remote in Ireland | Ireland but mostly on-site / long commute | Remote but awkward time zone |

## Tiers
- Tier A: 75+ (tailored CV + cover letter)
- Tier B: 60-74 (tailored summary and top bullets only)
- Tier C: under 60

## Search settings
- Sources: LinkedIn, IrishJobs.ie, Jobs.ie, Indeed Ireland, company Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby boards
- Recency: last 14 days
