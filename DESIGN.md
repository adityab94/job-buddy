# Design System: Job Buddy dashboard

## Overview

**Creative North Star: the calm greenhouse.** A job search is stressful; this page is the opposite. Frosted glass panes float over a soft gradient of butter, sage and apricot taken from The Buddy Social logo, and the one thing to do next is always the brightest pane. It is a working dashboard (Operate mode): familiar structure, plain language, brand carried by the wordmark, the palette and the glass.

The page is one offline HTML file generated by `scripts/dashboard.py`. Nothing loads from the network: the logo is an embedded alpha mask, icons are inline SVG, fonts are the system's own.

Anti-references: the grey admin grid of identical cards; a hero strip of big numbers; neumorphic grey; gradient text.

## Colors

Two grounds, one ink family, one brand green, and amber reserved for waiting.

- **Forest** `#4c7031` (logo green) and **Forest Ink** `#3b5a25`: brand marks, progress bars, focus rings, links, the logo mask.
- **Cream Ground** `#f7efda` with four washes (**Butter** `#fff0c4`, **Sage** `#b3d18c`, **Apricot** `#efc47a`, **Mint** `#cfe4bb`) and a blurred moss orb: the gradient field the glass frosts. The washes must be saturated enough to show through the glass; a pale field turns glass into plain cards.
- **Ink** `#1f2e17`, **Ink Soft** `#4f5d43`, **Ink Muted** `#5a654e`: text in three steps, all ≥4.5:1 on the lightest glass over the deepest wash.
- **Tier A** green, **Tier B** amber-brown, **Tier C** stone: tier chips only, always on their own tinted pill.
- **Waiting** `#86500a`: quiet applications and the "follow up" dot. Nothing else is amber.
- **Dark (night greenhouse)**: ground `#0e170d`, washes of deep moss and dim amber, cream ink `#f4ead2`, light sage brand `#a9cd86`. The logo recolours to cream through the same mask.

## Typography

One family, the platform sans, so the page feels native on Windows and Mac. The display voice belongs to the wordmark; type stays quiet beside it.

- **Title** 28px / 680, tracking -0.02em: "Your job search".
- **Heading** 17px / 650: pane and section headings. No eyebrows or kickers above headings.
- **Figure** 30px / 680, tabular numerals: the four counts and rates.
- **Body** 15px / 1.5; **Label** 12.5–13.5px for hints, lane heads and chips.

## Layout

- Container max 1240px, 24px gutters (16px on phones).
- First viewport: logo + title + one-sentence status on the left, search and theme toggle on the right; then **Next up** (≈2/3) beside **At a glance** (≈1/3), stretched to equal height. The pipeline starts at the fold.
- Then the pipeline as six stage lanes (horizontal scroll with snap on narrow screens, 78% lanes on phones so the next lane peeks), then two insight panes (scoring check, what to learn), then closed roles in a disclosure, then the signature footer.
- 40px between sections, 20px between panes, 14px row rhythm inside panes with hairline separators.
- Breakpoints: 980px (single column, tools below title), 560px (phone).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is glass, not shadow alone. Every pane has: translucent fill (`--glass` .46 light / .45 dark; `--glass-strong` .9 light / .82 dark for Next up, plus a faint brand ring), backdrop blur 24px with saturate 160%, a soft offset shadow with a 1px inner top highlight, a 170° sheen fading out by 42%, and a gradient rim (bright top-left fading to faint green) drawn with a masked 1px border. Hover lifts job cards 2px with a deeper shadow. Without backdrop-filter support, panes fall back to the strong fill.

## Shapes

Soft and rounded throughout: panes 22px, job cards and callouts 14px, chips, fields and buttons fully pill-shaped. Dots for status, 10px with a 5px halo ring. Dashed 1.5px outline only for empty pipeline lanes.

## Components

- **Next up list**: status dot (green = live stage, forest = to do, amber = waiting), company in 620 weight, role and action in Ink Soft, tier chip on the right (below the text on phones). At most eight items.
- **Counts**: a 2×2 grid divided by hairlines, figure plus plain-language label ("heard back with a next step", not "callback rate").
- **Job card**: company, role, tier chip, score, optional "quiet Nd" chip, "View ad" link with an out-arrow icon.
- **Tier bars**: 8px track in a brand tint, forest fill by callback rate, rate figure on the right, a verdict callout below.
- **Gap lift rows**: role on the left; score, tier chip, arrow, new score, new tier chip on the right.
- **Theme toggle**: round glass button with sun/moon SVG; light by default, choice remembered in the browser; the gradient crossfades over 0.7s via registered custom properties.
- **Footer signature**: small logo mask, "Created by **Aditya Bhatnagar** · The Buddy Social", and the privacy line.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the cream-to-sage logo palette and forest ink; it is a brand commitment.
- Do make the next action the brightest pane on the page.
- Do write labels a non-technical person understands on first read.
- Do theme browser surfaces: selection, focus rings, caret, scrollbars, `color-scheme` per theme.
- Don't load anything from the network: no web fonts, CDN scripts or remote images.
- Don't flatten the glass into opaque cards, or let the gradient wash out until the blur has nothing to frost.
- Don't add eyebrows, gradient text, hero metric strips or coloured side stripes.
- Don't use amber for anything except waiting.
